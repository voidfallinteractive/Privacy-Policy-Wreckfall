Privacy Policy for Wreckfall

Effective date: November 03, 2026

At Voidfall Interactive, accessible from our game portals and mobile applications, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Wreckfall and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Wreckfall is designed to be played without requiring you to submit personal identification information. However, when you play our game via platforms like CrazyGames or Google Play, these platforms may automatically collect technical data such as:

  • Device information and browser type
  • Anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports
  • Platform-specific user identifiers (managed securely by CrazyGames SDK or Google Play Services)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for purposes such as:

  • Providing, operating, and maintaining our game
  • Improving player experience and fixing performance bugs
  • Complying with platform requirements (e.g., SDK integration for web and mobile distribution)

3. Third-Party Services (SDKs)

Our game integrates distribution SDKs which may collect non-personal telemetry data according to their respective privacy policies:

  • CrazyGames SDK: Used for web-based distribution and session management. (CrazyGames Privacy Policy)
  • Google Play Services / Android: Used for mobile application deployment. (Google Privacy Policy)

4. Children's Privacy

Wreckfall does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, we advise you to review this page periodically for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: support@voidfall.com (or via your developer account details).
